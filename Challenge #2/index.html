My Green Story

It all started with a gate.

One ordinary day, while walking home from school with my classmates, something caught my attention. It was a green gate along the street. But it wasn't just any shade of green. There was something about its color that felt so comforting, peaceful, and beautiful to look at.

What made it even more fascinating was how elegant the gate looked despite the simplicity of the house behind it. Somehow, that particular shade made everything look different.

Curiosity got the better of me, so I searched for the color online. That's when I discovered its name: Olive Green - Secret Garden.

I never expected a simple walk home to introduce me to a color I would eventually become so fond of. From that day on, I started noticing green differently. Its shades, its beauty, and the peaceful feeling it brings slowly became something I couldn't get enough of.

Funny how something as ordinary as a gate can leave such an extraordinary impression. And just like that, my little love story with green began.
